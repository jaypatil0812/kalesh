# Message to send Instinct

Text this to Instinct once. It sets up the recurring hunt.

---

Hey, I need a recurring job. Every 3 hours from 9am to 1am IST, hunt X (Twitter) for fresh "kalesh": public, funny, low-stakes fights on Indian Twitter and tech Twitter. Add the best ones to my Google Sheet "KALESH Desk Inbox", tab "scout".

How to hunt (use your browser, logged into X):
- X search, Latest and Top, with queries like `lang:en min_faves:500 since:<yesterday>` plus words like "hot take", "ratio", "unpopular opinion", "founders", "VCs", "Bangalore", "Gurgaon", "70 hours", "layoffs", "biryani", "IPL", "Bollywood", "UPSC", "rent", "landlord", "dating apps". Also check Explore → Trending for India.
- Open the promising ones and look at the quote tweets. A real kalesh has people arguing back, not just agreeing.

It counts if it's from the last 48 hours AND (500+ likes on the original, OR 100+ quote tweets/replies arguing, OR two accounts with 10k+ followers going at each other, OR it became a meme the same day).

Never add: religion, caste, communal or regional hate; party politics, elections, government or military; deaths, tragedies, accidents, violence, crime, health; accusations like harassment, fraud or legal cases; private people or accounts under ~5k followers being dunked on; anyone who looks under 18; sexual content, slurs, doxxing, or screenshots with personal info. When unsure, skip it.

How to add a row (append below the last row of the "scout" tab, columns A to G only):
- A links: the tweet URL as https://x.com/<user>/status/<id>. Original first, then up to 3 key replies or quote tweets, separated by spaces.
- B title: optional short Hinglish meme headline (max 120 chars), or leave blank.
- C side: leave blank.
- D tags: 1 to 3 lowercase tags, comma separated.
- E why: one line saying who vs who, about what, and the numbers.
- F found_by: instinct
- G found_at: date and time in IST.
- Leave H, I, J empty. I approve rows myself.

Before adding, read column A of both the "scout" and "kalesh" tabs and skip any tweet already there. Max 5 rows per run. If nothing good turned up, add nothing and don't message me. Only message me if something is blowing up right now (5,000+ likes in a few hours).

Confirm the schedule back to me before the first run.
